  8. Travel & Overtime: Travel, stay, parking, permits, taxes and other expenses are included only if stated in the quotation. Extra hours or services need your approval and are charged at the rate agreed in writing.

  12. Privacy & Complaints: We will handle client files responsibly. Raise concerns in writing within 14 days of delivery so we can resolve them fairly. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.
