    Good to Know

    Frequently Asked Questions

    The questions couples ask us most — answered straight from our booking terms.

    How is my booking confirmed?

    Your booking is confirmed once you accept our terms, your date is available, and the agreed advance is received. Your package details and payment schedule are recorded in your quotation or invoice — see our Terms & Policies.

    How much advance do I need to pay?

    The advance is usually [Z]% of the package — [CLIENT TO CONFIRM]. The exact advance amount is agreed with you and recorded in your quotation before your date is locked. Unless agreed otherwise in writing, the remaining balance is due on or before the event date.

    When will I receive my photos and films?

    You receive the items listed in your package — edited photographs, films, albums or highlights. As a guide: highlight video usually in [X] days, full video & album usually in [Y] weeks — [CLIENT TO CONFIRM]. Your exact delivery time is confirmed in your quotation. If you need everything sooner, rush delivery is available as an add-on and may cost extra.

    Do you provide RAW photos or unedited footage?

    No — RAW photos, unedited footage and project files are not included unless agreed in writing. You receive professionally edited photographs and films, finished in the Sora Films style you see in our portfolio.

    Can I change my date or reschedule?

    Yes, tell us in writing as early as you can. A new date depends on availability, and any refund, cancellation fee or rescheduling charge follows your written quotation and applicable law. Costs already incurred may remain payable.

    What if my event runs longer than booked?

    Coverage is limited to the dates, venues, hours and services in your package. Extra hours or additional services are covered with your approval and charged at the rate agreed in writing — no surprises afterwards.

    Do you travel outside Gaya for weddings?

    Yes — we cover all weddings and events within 100 km of Gaya Ji, across Gaya, Aurangabad, Jehanabad, Arwal, Patna, Nalanda, Nawada, Rohtas, Sheikhpura, Bhojpur, Koderma, Chatra and Hazaribagh districts, including Bodh Gaya, Rajgir and Bihar Sharif. We also travel across India for outstation weddings. Travel, accommodation and other expenses are included only if specified in your quotation.

    Can I say no to my photos being used in your portfolio?

    Absolutely. On your booking agreement you choose Yes or No for promotional use of selected images and clips. Permission is completely optional, and declining it will never affect your booking or your package.

    Wedding Photography Terms & Conditions

    These are the terms that apply to every Sora Films booking — the same terms printed on the agreement you receive from our studio. Please read them carefully before confirming your booking.

    1Booking & Confirmation

    Booking is confirmed only after the client accepts these terms, the date is available, and the agreed advance is received. Package details and the payment schedule should be recorded in the quotation or invoice.

    2Payments

    The client agrees to pay the agreed package price on schedule. Unless agreed otherwise in writing, the remaining balance is due on or before the event date. Deliverables may be held until outstanding payments are cleared, subject to applicable law.

    3Cancellation & Rescheduling

    Cancellation or date changes must be communicated in writing. Any refund, cancellation fee, or rescheduling charge will follow the written quotation and applicable law. A new date depends on availability; costs already incurred may remain payable.

    4Coverage & Timings

    Coverage is limited to the dates, venues, hours, and services listed in the selected package. The client should share the event schedule and key-moment list in advance. Delays, restrictions, or events extending beyond booked hours may reduce coverage or incur agreed overtime charges.

    5Deliverables & Delivery

    Only the items listed in the package are included — such as edited photographs, films, albums, or highlights. The estimated delivery time is confirmed in your quotation. Rush delivery, extra edits, albums, prints, and additional films may cost extra.

    6Editing & Creative Style

    The client has reviewed Sora Films' portfolio and understands that editing, colour grading, composition, and storytelling reflect the studio's style. RAW photos, unedited footage, and project files are not included unless agreed in writing.

    7Client Responsibilities

    The client will provide accurate event details, venue access, required permissions, a point of contact, and reasonable meals and rest breaks for long coverage. The client is responsible for venue permissions and for notifying the studio about restrictions or no-photography areas.

    8Travel & Overtime

    Travel, accommodation, parking, permits, taxes, and other expenses are included only if specified in the quotation. Extra hours or additional services require client approval and will be charged at the rate agreed in writing.

    9Equipment & Uncontrollable Events

    Sora Films will take reasonable care of equipment and files. In case of illness, accident, equipment failure, severe weather, venue restrictions, or events beyond reasonable control, the studio will make reasonable efforts to arrange a suitable replacement or recover available work. Any liability is subject to applicable law.

    10Image Selection & Backup

    The studio selects final images according to professional standards. A shot list is a guide, not a guarantee that every person or moment will be captured. Files will be backed up using reasonable practices, but long-term storage is not guaranteed — the client should back up delivered files promptly.

    11Copyright & Personal Use

    Unless otherwise agreed in writing, copyright remains with the photographer, subject to applicable law. After full payment, the client receives a personal-use licence to download, print, and share delivered images for non-commercial purposes. Commercial use or major alterations require written permission.

    12Portfolio & Social Media Permission

    Sora Films may use selected images or clips for portfolio, website, social media, competitions, or promotion only if the client grants permission. Permission is optional and declining it will not affect the booking. Appropriate permissions for minors should be obtained where required.

    13Privacy

    The studio will take reasonable steps to handle client files responsibly. The client should notify the studio in writing about sensitive images or events that must not be shared. Both parties should protect private contact and payment information.

    14Complaints & Resolution

    Please raise concerns in writing within 14 days of delivery so both parties can try to resolve them fairly. Nothing in these terms limits rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.
