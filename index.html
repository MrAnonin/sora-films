How Booking Works

Four Steps to Your Film

Simple, transparent and in writing — the same process for every couple, from first enquiry to final delivery.

1

Enquiry & Date Check

Tell us your date and celebration on WhatsApp or the enquiry form. We check availability first and suggest the package that fits your day.

2

Quote & Booking Confirmed

You receive a written quotation with package details and the payment schedule. Your date is locked once you accept the terms and the agreed advance is received.

3

Shoot Day

Our team covers the dates, venues and hours in your package — photography, film and drone exactly as booked, so you can simply enjoy your day.

4

Editing & Delivery

We edit everything in the Sora Films style and deliver your photos, films and album. As a guide: highlight video usually in [X] days, full video & album usually in [Y] weeks — [CLIENT TO CONFIRM]. Your exact delivery time is confirmed in your quotation.

The full details are in our Terms & Policies — please read them before confirming your booking.
