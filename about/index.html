Welcome to Sora Films

Let Us Turn Your Moments Into Timeless Stories

Sora Films is a wedding and event photography & cinematography studio based in Gaya Ji, Bihar. From the first haldi ritual to the final vidaai, our team is ready to capture every beautiful moment of your special day — and all your events.

We blend candid photography, cinematic storytelling and sweeping drone shots into films and albums your family will treasure for generations.

We shoot weddings and events across Gaya Ji, Bodh Gaya, Rajgir, Nalanda, Patna, Aurangabad and the surrounding districts of Bihar — and outstation weddings anywhere in India on quotation travel terms.
